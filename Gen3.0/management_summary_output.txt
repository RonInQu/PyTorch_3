  Assumptions:
    - Current model: 85 training studies, GRU(32), 7s duration filter, single-frequency impedance
    - Conservative: Pressure used only as tiebreaker when impedance is ambiguous
    - Full GRU: Pressure features (min, std, frac<500, range over 2s window) added to feature vector
    - Clot F1 is the primary improvement target (largest current gap)
    - Net benefit estimates assume proportional reduction in harmful overrides

  Basis for estimates:
    - Wall: 88% of wall time is unambiguously P < 200 mmHg -> near-certain wall ID
    - Clot: 54% of clot time has P > 500 mmHg -> confirmed not-wall by pressure alone
    - Overlap (clot P < 500): resolved by impedance (clot Z median ~2700 ohm vs wall ~1900 ohm)
    - Combined rule: Wall = low P + moderate Z;  Clot = high Z (regardless of P)

  Risk factors:
    - Promedica case shows 60% clot time with P < 500 (worst case for pressure-only)
    - This is resolved by impedance, but demonstrates pressure alone is insufficient
    - Gen3.0 data does not yet include simultaneous impedance -- estimates assume
      similar impedance separation as in 85 existing training studies
